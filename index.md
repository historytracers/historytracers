# History Tracers

History Tracers is a free, open-source educational project that teaches mathematics, science, history, literature and genealogy through culture, games and primary sources. Content is available in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.

## Explore

- [First steps](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=first_steps_menu): Introductory mathematics for children and beginners.
- [Universe](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=physics): Physics and astronomy lessons.
- [The Origin of Matter](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=chemistry): Chemistry lessons.
- [The Origin of Life](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=biology): Biology lessons.
- [Atlas](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=atlas): Geography, maps and human migration.
- [General History](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=history): History lessons and primary sources.
- [Genealogical Trees](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=families): Family history and genealogy.
- [Understanding texts](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=literature): Literature and reading lessons.
- [Mathematical Games](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=math_games): Free educational games and applications.
- [Gallery](https://www.historytracers.org/index.html?page=gallery): Images and museum collections used in the lessons.
